## Residual Risk Statements

1. Incorrect classification or routing by an upstream agent may delay or mis-prioritise cases. Mitigation: human review of high-severity items + daily sample checks.
2. Research or knowledge-retrieval agent may surface outdated or incorrect content. Mitigation: version-controlled knowledge base + policy check by a review agent + human override.
3. Generated content may contain tone, factual or policy violations. Mitigation: dedicated review step before release + mandatory human approval on high-risk items.

## Decision Gates

1. System Owner must sign the full accountability package before first production deployment.
2. Any material change to agent prompts, tools, or knowledge sources that affects high-risk routing requires re-sign-off by System Owner and the relevant Review / Oversight owner.
3. Go-live is blocked until residual-risk statements have named owners and review frequencies.

## Human Oversight Points

- High-severity or high-urgency items require mandatory human approval before action is taken.
- Weekly sample review of completed cases by a senior human reviewer.
- Monthly review of the residual-risk register by the System Owner.
- Prompt and knowledge-base changes are logged and reviewed within 5 business days.
